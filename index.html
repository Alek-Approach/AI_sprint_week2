This week's goals

  1. Name the five parts of a prompt and find what is missing in a weak prompt.
  2. Write a prompt for a real work or study task with all five parts.

    Activity 4 — Instruction Language Lab

    Vague words give vague output. Choose the most precise instruction for each vague one.

    Language bank

    Imperatives (clear and direct)

    • Write… / List… / Compare…
    • Rewrite… / Summarize…
    • Avoid… / Do not…

    Polite requests

    • Could you…?
    • Please…
    • I'd like you to…

    Tone descriptors

    • formal · neutral · friendly
    • persuasive · encouraging
    • warm but professional

    Length descriptors

    • in under 100 words
    • in 3 bullet points
    • in one sentence · a 2-minute script

    Activity 6 — Pair Prompt Challenge

    Use the real work or study task you chose for homework in Week 1. Build a strong prompt, test it, get feedback, and improve it.

    Phase 1 — Plan

    Privacy check: no real names, salaries, client data, grades or company secrets. Replace them with [Name], [Company], [Amount].
    Three success criteria (a good output will…)

    Phase 2 — Build & Test

    Driver types and tests; navigator suggests changes. Switch roles after about 17 minutes. Rate each output against your success criteria.

    Phase 3 — Swap & Peer-Evaluate

    Copy your best prompt and send it to the other pair. Run their prompt in your AI assistant, then score it.
